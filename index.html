How it works

From listing to handover in four steps

  1. Create an account

    Sign up with your email to join your campus community.

  2. List or browse

    Add a photo and price in a couple of minutes, or browse the newest listings.

  3. Contact the seller

    Found something? Get in touch to ask questions and agree a price.

  4. Meet on campus

    Arrange payment and pickup together, somewhere you both know.

Prototype status

An honest look at what works today

UnivBid is a portfolio project, not a live marketplace yet.

Works today

  • Sign up and sign in
  • Create listings with a photo, then edit or remove them
  • Browse the newest listings, 12 at a time
  • A dashboard with your listings, items sold and earnings

Not switched on yet

  • Online payments: you contact the seller instead
  • Bidding, despite the name
  • Email verification for new accounts
