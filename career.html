Our story

Built around student life.

Every semester, good things get thrown away or left behind while other students go out and buy the same things new. UnivBid is a concept for keeping them on campus instead.

See how it works Share feedback
What we care about

A marketplace works best when it feels close to home.

  • Local by design

    Buyers and sellers share a campus, so handovers are short walks, not shipping labels.

  • Less waste

    Textbooks, lamps and desks get a second semester instead of a trip to the bin.

  • Honest and safe

    Clear labels for what's real and what's a sample, and user content that's never rendered as code.

About the project

A portfolio prototype

UnivBid is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on Firebase, with a tested Stripe payments backend that isn't switched on yet. See the prototype status for what works today.
